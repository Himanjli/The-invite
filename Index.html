The Backstory

Our Story

Every great love story has a spark, a few inside jokes, and plenty of #RishabhKiHimmat. Here is how ours unfolded.

Chapter 1 The Spark

How It Started

Somewhere between a casual hello and an unexpectedly long conversation, we realized we clicked instantly. Within months, we were inseparable—neither of us said a word, yet we quietly preferred each other's company over the whole world. On a chilly movie date, my hands were freezing; he took them in his, warmed them up, and melted my heart right along with them. He may not always find the words, but his actions speak so much louder.

✦ Actions > words, always
Chapter 2 The Secret

The Plot Twist

Countless food debates, shared playlists, childhood stories, and late-night life talks made it clear that life is infinitely better when your favorite person is also your best friend. For a long time, we kept our relationship under wraps while we got to know each other deeply. Of course, our friends eventually started guessing—and whenever I needed Rishabh to do something, I’d tease him with “main tera bhaanda fod dungi.” Still our favorite inside joke to date.

✦ Status: officially inseparable
Chapter 3 The “Yes!”

The Proposal

It took a whole lot of #RishabhKiHimmat, one heartfelt question, and a very happy “yes!” After two years of dating, we knew we belonged together. When we told our parents, all four of them didn't hesitate to put their kids' happiness above log kya kahenge. It truly feels like the stars aligned for us—and we couldn't be more grateful to be surrounded by so much love.

✦ Stars aligned & blessed with love
Chapter 4 The Big Day

Forever Begins

And now, here we are—finally ready to walk together for life. From quiet moments and inside jokes to dreaming up a future of our own, every step has led us to March 11th. Starting this new chapter wouldn't feel complete without the people who shaped our story, so nothing would mean more to us than having you by our side—for the laughter, the celebration, and most of all, your presence and blessings.
